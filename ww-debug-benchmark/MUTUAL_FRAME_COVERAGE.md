# Mutual dogfight interior frame coverage

The mutual verifier requires at least one valid frame for each aircraft at every integer tick strictly between the unique scene-start and scene-completion ticks. A valid frame has a nonnegative integer tick, finite nonnegative numeric speed and a boolean ground state. Duplicate frames cannot fill a missing tick; all recorded frames inside the scene window remain subject to the existing speed and ground checks. An empty interior or any incomplete interior fails verification, and both aircraft's reported combat-window minimum speeds and ground counts become `null`. The separately reported interior counts count ticks with at least one valid frame, so an absent or malformed frame is uncovered.

The scene's initial opponent frame may come from the fixture's explicit `applyControl` before the opening move, not a scheduled entity tick. Cleanup may discard the opponent before it can emit a frame in the completion tick. Those two boundary ticks are therefore excluded from this specific coverage condition; their safety is not established by passing it. Missing rows may reflect entity scheduling, logging failures or other causes: incomplete coverage means unverified, not a diagnosis.

Complete interior coverage is a necessary evidence condition, not proof that both aircraft were sampled simultaneously, that all transitions between samples were safe, that bounding boxes did not touch, or that the firing and collision conditions were met. The producer can write multiple frames in one tick, and file ordering or same-tick labels alone do not establish sub-tick order. Separate verifier changes are needed to check telemetry integrity, identity and tick ordering; this focused change is based on the frame-integrity branch and must be revalidated when stacked with them.
